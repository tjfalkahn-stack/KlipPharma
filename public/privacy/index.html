KlipPharma Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Friendly Partners AI (“KlipPharma,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use KlipPharma.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, authentication information, subscription status, and account preferences; content and files you choose to upload; project settings and edits; technical information such as device, browser, IP address, logs, and security events; usage information about how you interact with KlipPharma; and information received from third-party platforms when you authorize an integration.

2. Connected Social Accounts

If you connect TikTok, YouTube, or another supported service, KlipPharma may receive information permitted by the scopes you authorize, such as account identifiers, profile information, authorized video information, publishing capabilities, and performance information. We use this information to provide the connected features you request.

We do not obtain your third-party account password through OAuth authorization. Access tokens and refresh tokens are treated as sensitive credentials and should be stored and processed server-side using appropriate safeguards.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and operate KlipPharma; authenticate users; process and edit media; generate AI-assisted recommendations; provide analytics and monetization-readiness tools; perform authorized publishing or upload actions; maintain account and billing functions; detect fraud, abuse, and security threats; troubleshoot and improve the service; communicate with users; and comply with legal obligations.

4. TikTok Data

When you connect TikTok, we access TikTok data only within the permissions you authorize and for the purpose of providing the requested KlipPharma features. We do not sell TikTok user data. We do not use TikTok credentials to publish content without user authorization. Publishing actions are initiated through the user-facing KlipPharma workflow and remain subject to TikTok's rules and permissions.

5. Your Videos and Creator Content

Your uploaded videos and creator assets remain your content. KlipPharma may process, transcode, analyze, temporarily copy, store, or transmit content as necessary to provide editing, AI, rendering, storage, and distribution features. We do not claim ownership of your creator content.

6. AI Processing

Content or text may be processed by AI service providers when necessary to deliver features such as transcription, clip recommendations, translation, dubbing, content analysis, or creator assistance. We limit information sent to service providers to what is reasonably necessary for the requested functionality.

7. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that support hosting, storage, media processing, AI processing, payments, security, analytics, communications, and other infrastructure; with third-party platforms when you instruct us to connect, upload, or publish; when required by law or valid legal process; or in connection with a business transaction subject to appropriate protections. We do not sell personal information for money.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Connected-platform tokens may be deleted or invalidated when an integration is disconnected, subject to technical and legal requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security, and users are responsible for protecting their account credentials.

10. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also disconnect supported third-party integrations and revoke permissions through the applicable platform.

11. Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of your KlipPharma account or eligible personal information by contacting support@klippharma.com.

12. Children's Privacy

KlipPharma is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13. International Processing

Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service, integrations, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required.

15. Contact

Privacy questions and requests may be sent to support@klippharma.com.
